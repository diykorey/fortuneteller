How it works

Tested on the past: where a market went the same way most of the time over a stretch of time, and that kept coming true when tested on history, the fortune teller says it will again.

Our guess: a hunch from today's news, with no record behind it yet.

Each says which way, not by how much. Every one is kept and checked when its time is up, and the two kinds are scored apart. For fun, not financial advice.
