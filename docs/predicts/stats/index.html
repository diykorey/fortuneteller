Every guess the fortune teller has made, with its reasoning, its grade once its end date passed, and its own look back. 0 came true, 0 did not, 25 still open. Guesses made on one evening move together, so a kind of reasoning is judged by its evenings: an evening counts once, right if most of its guesses came true. A kind is promoted when it has 30 evenings, at least 60 in 100 mostly right, and a chance of under 1 in 100 that a coin would do as well. For fun, not financial advice.
