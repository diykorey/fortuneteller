Step 44's first-visitor review (docs/steps/readers.md). A fresh reader, given only this prompt.

The prompt:

You are a first-time visitor who clicked a LinkedIn link. You know nothing about this project.
Fetch https://diykorey.github.io/fortuneteller/scoreboard/ (also fetch
https://diykorey.github.io/fortuneteller/scoreboard/data.json if the page loads its content from
it, since the page is rendered by JavaScript). Do not read any repository files or docs. Change
nothing.

Answer, numbered, briefly and concretely:
1. Within the first screen, what do you think this page is and what it offers you? Quote the
   first ~5 lines a visitor sees.
2. What is the single most interesting thing on the page for an ordinary person (not a trader)?
   Quote it.
3. Every word, abbreviation or number you could not understand without help (list them).
4. What would make you leave within 10 seconds?
5. What would make you come back next week, or share it? Is there anything dated and upcoming
   ("on Wednesday X is released, here is what followed the last N times")?
6. The three changes that would most raise the chance a stranger stays and returns, most
   important first, each in one sentence.
Report facts from the page only; say "none" where you found nothing.

The baseline, 2026-11-21 (page written 2026-11-19; rendered in headless Chrome at 1366x800):

   First lines: "FortuneTeller / Data to 2026-11-19; written 2026-11-19. / Every call made on a
   release day, graded on the market's close; every event's verdicts for every market; every
   link's record on the replay. ... / Ask the fortune teller / Entertainment, not financial
   advice. Pick a kind of event ..." The default pick is "a Fed rate cut (40)": every row visible
   reads "can't say".
2. Behind the menu: "After a CEO's conduct in question, the company against the S&P 500 on the

3. Page words: call, release day, link, replay, "replayed, it held", verdict, cells, band, grade,
   void, silent, moves / unclear / doesn't / tracks / holds / too few. Statistics: p, p < 0.01,
   binomial, ratio, corr, hit rate, "chance alone passes about 9.18". Events: CPI, NFP, Fed, RET,
